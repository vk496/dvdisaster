\section{RS04 tag and CRC sector format}
\label{rs04-tag}

A tag sector holds the following 2048-byte structure; for $B > 2048$ the
remaining bytes of the sector are zero. All integers are little-endian
(section \ref{rs04-conventions}). Every field is at an offset which is a
multiple of its size, so the C structure below has no padding on any
common ABI.

\medskip
{\small
\begin{tabular}{|r|r|l|l|p{7.4cm}|}
\hline
Offset & Size & Type & Field & Contents \\
\hline
0 & 12 & char & $cookie$ & {\tt *dvdisaster*} (no terminating zero) \\
12 & 4 & char & $method$ & {\tt RS04} \\
16 & 2 & u16 & $version$ & 1 (section \ref{rs04-versions}) \\
18 & 1 & u8 & $layoutVariant$ & 0 \\
19 & 1 & u8 & $flags$ & bit 0: created by a development release;
  bit 1: created by a release candidate. The user is hinted that the data
  comes from a non-stable dvdisaster version. Other bits are zero. \\
20 & 2 & u16 & $sectorBytes$ & $B$: 2048, 2324 or 2352 \\
22 & 2 & u16 & $tagInterval$ & $T$: 2, 4, \ldots, 256 \\
24 & 4 & u32 & $creatorVersion$ & dvdisaster version which created the set;
  the decimal value 102345 means version 10.23.45 \\
28 & 4 & u32 & $selfCRC$ & Crc32 of bytes 0--2047 computed with this field
  set to zero \\
32 & 8 & bytes & $setID$ & random; identical in all tags of the set \\
40 & 8 & u64 & $dataBytes$ & length of the protected data in bytes;
  $S = \lceil dataBytes/B \rceil$ \\
48 & 8 & u64 & $layerSectors$ & $L$ \\
56 & 4 & u32 & $dataLayers$ & $k$ \\
60 & 4 & u32 & $eccLayers$ & $m$ \\
64 & 8 & u64 & $ownPosition$ & relative position of this tag, $S + gT$ for
  tag $g$ \\
72 & 16 & bytes & $dataMD5$ & MD5 of the $dataBytes$ bytes of data \\
88 & 1020 & u32[255] & $crc$ & Crc32 of the following payload sectors
  (section \ref{rs04-tags}); entries $T-1 \ldots 254$ and entries beyond the
  last payload sector are zero \\
1108 & 940 & & & zero \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
}
\medskip

A CRC sector holds 512 little-endian Crc32 values (2048 bytes, section
\ref{rs04-crc}), followed by $B - 2048$ zero bytes.
